Finance / Financial Empowerment

Mellody Hobson


Co‑CEO & President of Ariel Investments, one of the largest Black‑owned investment firms in the U.S. (Barron's)


She emphasizes financial literacy, inclusion, and diversity in corporate governance.


Fun fact / quote idea: She’s served on numerous boards and commissions, and she frames investing as a pathway to empowerment.


Thasunda Brown Duckett


CEO of TIAA (a financial services firm serving academics, researchers, and governmental institutions). (Billionaire Daily News)


Earlier, she led large-scale operations in consumer banking.


Fun fact / quote idea: She is one of only a few Black women to lead a Fortune 500 company.


Suzanne Shank


CEO & President of Siebert Williams Shank & Co, one of the largest Black‑owned investment banking firms in the U.S. (Ellevest)


She began her career as an engineer before moving into finance.


Fun fact / quote idea: She emphasizes mentorship: “The most important thing we can do right now is give a hand up and provide mentorship for other women.” (Ellevest)


Tonya Rapley


Founder of My Fab Finance, a financial education platform for women, especially women of color. (FunTimes Magazine)


Creator of the “#BanishTheBalance” challenge to help people reduce debt.


Fun fact / quote idea: She started with little capital and now helps thousands improve their finances.


Carla Harris


Vice Chairman, Wealth Management at Morgan Stanley. (CNBC)


Known for speaking and writing on leadership, networking, and navigating the corporate world.


Fun fact / quote idea: She often encourages younger professionals to invest not just in money, but in relationships and reputation.
